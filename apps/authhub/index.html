TOTP and HOTP

Time-based and counter-based codes. SHA1, SHA256, SHA512; 6 or 8 digits.

Add with QR

Scan with the camera or enter the secret manually. The service and account labels stay yours.

Biometric lock

Unlock with Face ID, Touch ID or the device passcode. The app locks when it goes to the background.

Code masking

Hide codes so they are not exposed in screenshots or over your shoulder. Tap to copy.

Three layouts

List, compact list or grid. The newest code is always at the top.

Stays on device

Tokens in the Keychain, records on your device. No account, server or cloud.

Göz atmak için üç görünüm

Aynı kodlar; ekranına ve kullanımına göre liste, kompakt liste veya ızgara.

Three ways to glance

The same codes; list, compact list or grid depending on your screen and habit.

Gizlilik önce gelir

Jetonlar cihazının Anahtar Zinciri’nde tutulur. Gizli anahtarlar hiçbir sunucuya gönderilmez.

Privacy first

Tokens are kept in your device’s Keychain. Secret keys are never sent to a server.

About ads: A banner ad is shown at the bottom of the home screen and cannot be turned off. You can only change the tracking permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you decline, non-personalized ads are shown.
